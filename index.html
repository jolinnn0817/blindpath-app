How this demonstration works
  1. Submit an OKU verification request.
  2. The status changes to Pending.
  3. A demo reviewer can simulate approval or rejection below.
In a real service, verification must use an authorised process. This demo does not contact JKM, MyGOV or any government database. Do not upload identity documents to this prototype.
