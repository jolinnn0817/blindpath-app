Welcome to BlindPath

Making townships more accessible through voice-first navigation, community reporting and inclusive mobility support.

BlindPath is a prototype designed to help users explore routes and document accessibility problems.

What would you like to do?

Our mission

BlindPath aims to support a more inclusive township by helping people plan routes, report blocked tactile paving and share accessibility concerns.

Safety reminder: GPS and map data do not detect physical obstacles, confirm tactile paving, verify traffic-light signals or guarantee that a crossing is safe.
