BlindPath aims to make everyday journeys more accessible through route planning, voice guidance and community reporting of tactile path problems.

Our goal

Help people with visual impairments plan journeys and highlight accessibility gaps. Always use your own judgement and follow local road-safety rules.

Community & Accessibility

BlindPath is designed to support a community-led approach to more accessible townships.

How it could help

  • Identify places where tactile paths may be missing.
  • Highlight blocked or damaged tactile paths.
  • Help planners understand recurring accessibility concerns.
  • Encourage more inclusive public spaces.
In this prototype, reports stay on the device where they were created. Shared reporting, official verification, sensors and smart-cane integration are future development ideas.
