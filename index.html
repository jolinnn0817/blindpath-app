BlindPath

Navigate with greater confidence.

BlindPath is an accessibility-focused navigation prototype designed to make journey information easier to access through voice guidance and community reports.

Safety notice: This prototype cannot detect obstacles, verify tactile paths, or confirm whether a crossing signal is safe. Always use your established mobility aids and trusted crossing procedures.
