Accessible mobility

Move with more confidence.

BlindPath is an accessibility-focused navigation prototype designed to help users plan journeys, explore reported tactile-path issues, and access spoken route guidance.

Important: This prototype does not verify whether a route is physically accessible or safe. Always use your usual mobility aids and follow local safety guidance.
